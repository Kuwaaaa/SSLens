The web feels different when someone has been here before.

Most pages are silent after publication. Readers arrive alone, think alone, and leave no trace for the next person.

Lumen adds a small social layer that respects the page. A Lens can attach to this exact passage, persist over time, and become part of the page's memory.

Comments, questions, references, and tiny reactions accumulate where they belong: beside the thing people were reading.

When a passage gets dense, Lumen can show overlap heat without turning the page into a wall of motion.

Full mode reveals more of the room, but the reader decides when to open that layer.
